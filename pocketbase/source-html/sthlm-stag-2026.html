Reference

On the day

⚠️ Saturday is a big day in Stockholm
Royal Golden Wedding (50th anniversary) on Sat 13. Procession + concert in Kungsträdgården, plus Foo Fighters at Strawberry Arena, plus Rosendal Garden Party (Djurgården). Book restaurants & taxis early — the city will be heaving.
Hotel
Scandic, Norrmalm. 2 min walk from Stockholm Central Station.
Airport → Hotel
Arlanda Express train · 20 min · ~£26 return · much faster than bus or taxi. Or Flygbussarna coach for ~£10 if budget matters.
Getting around
SL travel card: 24-hour pass ~£11. Covers metro, tram, bus, AND the ferry to Djurgården. Buy in the SL app on arrival.
Money
Stockholm is basically cashless. Bring cards only, no cash needed. Pubs, taxis, museums all card. Watch out — Sweden is expensive: pint ~£8, cocktail ~£14, dinner mains ~£25.
Daylight
Sunset around 10pm, full daylight till 11pm, never really gets properly dark. Will mess with your sleep — eye masks are gold.
Tipping
Not expected. Round up at restaurants if service was great. No bar tips.
